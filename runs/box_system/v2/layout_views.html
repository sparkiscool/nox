Box structure v2: four layouts

Nine bays (4 Overseer, 5 Scout) on the swap arm's ring, grip line Y 167.1, each variant cut through its own concept meshes (to scale, mm, W frame). Bay bodies are design spaces for the topology run, not final shapes. A is the DEFAULT. The clash lists come from system_check, with the catch's funnel v8 plain file as it is on main.

  • arm: hub, motors, parked heads
  • arm: drone insert lanes
