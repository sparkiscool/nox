Box structure v2: four layouts

Nine bays (4 Overseer, 5 Scout) on the swap arm's ring, grip line Y 165, each variant cut through its own concept meshes (to scale, mm, W frame). Bay bodies are design spaces for the topology run, not final shapes. A is the DEFAULT. The clash lists come from system_check; the catch's −Y panel notch (panel v9, hub) and funnel v8 clear the catch pairs.
