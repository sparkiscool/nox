Heads v7 grabber concept

Nox swap arm, both heads. The plug and unplug loads (60 to 74 N push, 50 to 57 N pull) go through the grip pocket, the blades and carbon rods into the head body. Springs hold the grip; the servo only opens it. Section through the tab line, to scale (4 px per mm), latch 258becf grabber interface.
