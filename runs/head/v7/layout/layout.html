Heads v7 layout

Nox swap arm heads A (DS3218) and B (MG995, mirrored). Sections cut from the layout's solids, to scale. The servo lies under the grip; one crossed spring closes both blades and holds the cartridge with the servo off; the cam opens them; the pull goes through carbon rods into the body, never the servo.

Changes from the system pass's concept

  • carriage plate top Y 72.75 -> 74.35 under the cartridge (accepted by the arm system 24 Oct, with M5 x 20 axles): the upper-bound FE of the whole design space reads 0.73..1.03 at 72.75; the plate and the column's root also grow below u 0 where the arm leaves room (to u -18, the park bisector)
  • insert-lane keep-out at the drone, lower bound Z 111.5 -> 95.0 (head and grabber) and Z 100 -> 89 (carriage lane), accepted by the arm system 24 Oct (servo bottom u -12.5, horn u -15 -> Z 96.3 at the commanded insert): nothing of the catch (Z >= 154) or the structure is there
  • open blade position inner faces +-11.0 (concept 10.5): a 0.8 rib into the grip pocket must clear the latched tab's outer face (10) on the way in (the latch's +-11 open; the tab's 1.0 chamfer cams a 1.0 head offset); stroke 5.0 per blade
  • one crossed spring (15 N squeezed per blade, L 44 -> 34) instead of one spring per blade: the -X blade has 13 mm to the park bisector (the two parked heads), where a 15 N spring would need > 950 MPa (alternatives)
  • two inward stops on the body take a load across the cartridge (43.5 N, receiver gaps closed); the system pass had no X path
  • blades 10 wide (Y 160..170; the latch's 13): 1.5 clear of the hall package at Y 157 (the tab magnet's spot, z_T + 8); the rib (7.7 wide) is unchanged
  • 623ZZ roller followers on the carriers (no sliding at the cam): the servo needs about 2.5 kg.cm peak (concept 1.98 average with mu 0.3 on 19.5 N)
  • the m1 rack is its own bar (printed flat, teeth in the layers, epoxied to the web): the body prints on its side (+x), where the column's bending and the plate's lie in the layers
