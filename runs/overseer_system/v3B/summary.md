## Method
- **Layout B** (Spark): the swap set's CG 30 behind the thrust axis, the catch centre on the latch set centre (24.8 behind). The motor square moves until the CG sits on it; the wheel height steps down until the funnel fits with the latch v1 set (cartridge, pack, receiver, XT60); the L2 moves out until its scan is clear.
- **L2 (Spark 24 Oct)**: the 25 base is held and caged, the head is exposed, and no part of the drone enters the scan: 96° about the axis from the top of the base (the negative-angle mode, DEFAULT; the axis 15° below +x). A printed square shroud (cage, TPU) stands 1 off the base's 75 square faces, 2.5 wall, from 1.8 under the base plane to 17 up: the highest its corners can stand outside the scan (1.9 clear). The 8 of base above it is exposed with the head; the L2 is rolled 90° about its axis so its cable leaves left through a closed window in the shroud.
- **Cage** (`cage_opt2.py`): 12-DOF beam FE; crash rigid 35 g x the drone mass (DEFAULT, L) in 14 directions on every cage node within 3 of the leading support (64 hit points); members sized to ≤ 1 / 1.5 of their limit in crash so a cage node (the fuse) fails first; L2 crash 35 g, handling 50 N, catch and floor landing 6 g, latch 175 N, receiver misuse; L2 and belly modes ≥ 100 Hz. Per-rod OD caps: prop guard 12, funnel faces 1.0 and the keep-alive band, motor bells, camera boxes, the plate stack, the L2 body, dome and scan, prop wash (≤ 10 od where ≥ 15 of a rod lies in a disk in plan, DEFAULT). Thick wall and solid carbon at the capped ODs (10/6, 8/4, 6/3 tube, 10 solid; DEFAULT) only where a cap binds. Fully stressed sizing per run, take-back pass.
- **Carbon break-even per physical rod** (CLAUDE.md): a rod runs on through its printed cross nodes; one under 33 is printed. Only the two 16 nose rails print (struts in the collar); the shortest carbon rod is 50.8 (mount hi), 174 rods.
- **Balance**: moving the motor square moves the cage, the L2 and the set with it; only the plates stay, so the balance point is sensitive to the cage mass. The final stage brackets the square position, sizing the cage cold at each point, weighing and re-sizing until the drone mass holds, and takes the first point with |CG - thrust centre| ≤ 1.5 and the frame passing (3 points tried). The mast stands at x ≥ 119 (clear of the plate stack and the front hub block) when the thrust centre is further back.
