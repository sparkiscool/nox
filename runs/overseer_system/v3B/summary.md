## Method
- **Layout B** (Spark): the swap set's CG 30 (Spark) behind the thrust axis, the catch centre on the latch set centre 24.8 (C) behind. The motor square moves until the CG sits on it; the wheel height steps down until the funnel fits with the latch v1 set; the L2 moves out until its scan is clear.
- **L2** (Spark 24 Oct): the base is held and caged, the head exposed, no part of the drone in the scan: 96° (DEFAULT, negative-angle mode) about the axis from the top of the 25 (Spark) base, axis 15° (Spark) below +x. A printed TPU square shroud stands 1 (C) off the base's 75 (M) square faces, 2.5 (C) wall, from 1.8 (C) under the base plane to 17 (C) up: the highest its corners stand outside the scan (1.9 clear, C). The top 8 (C) of the base is exposed with the head (DEFAULT, in Spark's open items). The L2 is rolled 90° (DEFAULT) so its cable leaves left through a closed window in the shroud. Probes treat the head and that band as exempt protected parts that shadow the base; cage first gives the base an opening of 45° (C) about its axis, min 0, where the head leads it by ≥ 19.5 (C, review).
- **Cage** (`cage_opt2.py`): 12-DOF beam FE. Crash rigid 35 g (DEFAULT, L) x the drone mass in 14 directions on every cage node within 3 (DEFAULT) of the leading support, the L2 head included (it leads nose-first). Breaking order per crash case: every member ≤ 1 / 1.5 (DEFAULT, L) of its limit and the touching node's sockets ≤ 1, so each touching node is a drawn fuse rated between the hit and 1.5 x it. L2 crash, handling 50 N, catch and floor landing 6 g, latch 175 N, receiver misuse; L2 and belly modes ≥ 100 Hz (L). Per-rod OD caps: prop guard 12 (DEFAULT), funnel faces and top edge 1.0 for the rod and for its socket bosses, motor bells, camera boxes, plate stack, L2 body, dome and scan, prop wash ≤ 10 od (DEFAULT). Nodes and hubs are drawn and weighed from their sockets (a core round the largest bore, one boss per rod: bore OD + 0.3, 3 wall, 23 from the centre; C / E).
- **Carbon**: 8 stock types (DEFAULT): 3.0, 6/4, 8/6, 10/8, 10/6, 10 solid, 16/14, 20/18; 10/6 and 10 solid only where the wash cap binds. 7 types did not carry the rear skirt; all 14 save 4 g (F, comparison run). One section per physical rod (a rod runs on through its cross nodes); the 33 (CLAUDE.md) break-even per physical rod: only the two 16 (C) nose rails print.
- **Balance**: the final stage brackets the motor square position, sizing the cage cold at each point, weighing and re-sizing until the drone mass holds, and takes the first point with |CG - thrust centre| ≤ 1.5 (DEFAULT) and the frame passing (3 points). The mast stands at x ≥ 119 (C) when the thrust centre is further back, printed with the front hub block (21 apart, C). The rear hub clamps the bottom plate's rear edge (DEFAULT): under the plate its socket bosses reached the funnel lip.

## What the extra grams buy
The cage is ~1606 g (E) against ~800 g in the first v3B pass: that pass sized crash on a 10 stroke nobody drew (691 N) and drew nodes as small cubes. The cage now carries a hard 35 g hit with a fuse margin in every case, with nodes drawn round their real sockets. T/W falls to ~1.6 (E): a flight cost for Spark.
