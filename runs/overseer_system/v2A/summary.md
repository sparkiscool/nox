# Overseer system v2A (layout A, 24 Oct)

Spark 24 Oct: "this cage is way too flimsy ... the lidar mounted tight ... the cage can take impact and the bottom can land without toppling over, run a serious design optimization"; "weight doesn't matter now, just focus on design and keep it light"; "two cameras ... both pointing forward angled so that they capture as much of the lidar's view as possible".

`SYS_RESIZE=2 SYS_TOPO=tip_rails,chin,nose_x SYS_VIDEO=1 SYS_WRITE_ASM=1 python3 nox/overseer/overseer_system.py runs/overseer_system/v2A` (~40 min; video not committed). One script lays out the whole drone round the cage, balances it, sizes the cage on its own final geometry, checks it and writes the assembly file.

## Method
- **Layout A** (DEFAULT; alt B pack 30 aft, `runs/overseer_system/v1B`): pack on the thrust axis (balance rule). The motor square moves ahead of the plates until the CG sits on it; the wheel height steps down until the funnel fits; the L2 moves out until its field is clear and the V rods keep 12 off the front disks.
- **Cage** (`nox/overseer/cage_opt2.py`): 12-DOF beam FE, rigid joints, carbon rods 3.0 solid to 20/18 tube, PETG arms with 4 carbon spars. Cases (`dimensions.md` L): crash 35 g x 3.0 kg (E) at the touching node in 14 directions, L2 crash 35 g, handling 50 N per node, catch landing and floor landing 6 g, latch 100 N, swap pull 50 N; deflection ≤ 9, L2 and belly (down camera, flow) modes ≥ 100 Hz. Geometric constraints with per-rod size caps: prop guard (12 in plan, 12 off the swept band), funnel (1.0 off the faces, inside the 168 opening at ±5° yaw), motor bells (1.0), hook latch plungers (wheel ring t6), skid t10, rear ties t8. Fully stressed sizing per rod run, symmetric left / right, strain-energy stiffening for the modes. Topology: 1024 option sets searched on the system geometry whenever the warm-started sizing fails (`cage_search.json`: 62 of 1024 feasible on a geometry within ~10 mm of this one, before the camera obstacles and the rear hub move; its best set, tip rails + chin + nose cross, is the one sized here and passes). The layout and the sizing alternate; at the end the layout is re-balanced with the settled sizes and the sizes only stiffen (warm start) until both hold. Geometric obstacles: prop disks, funnel, motor bells, the two cameras (plate + board, lens).
- **Two front cameras**: `runs/overseer_cams/v1` (mirror pair, 54.7 % of the L2 field).

## Result (the final layout, `checks.txt`)
- Motors 213.7 ahead of the plate centre (front arms r 380, rear r 158, C); L2 base (507.3, 0, 42), field clear (closest 1.0 behind it), thin nose rods block 0.1 % of it.
- Cage topology: rings, posts, spokes, V, wheel ring, struts, nose box with rear-bottom bar, rear tower, rear skirt, belly frame + skid (fixed) + tip rails, lower keels, crossed keels, chin rods, arm spars, nose cross, top spider. Rods 882 g + spars 41 g (C), ~1007 g with nodes (E); 138 rods, 3.0 solid x 13 up to 20/18 x 20. Carbon only where its length earns it: the shortest rods are the rear skirt ties (37) and the skid legs (44), all ≥ 33 (C, CLAUDE.md).
- Frame model (F): worst rod 0.99, buckling 0.96, sockets 0.90, arm 0.11; touching node ≤ 4.9 (≤ 9); L2 mode 101 Hz, cage first mode 100 Hz, belly (down camera, flow) ≥ 100 Hz; L2 crash 0.06, handling 0.48, latch 0.63; violation 0.000. Frame and mounts stay under the cage (arm 0.11, L2 0.06 vs node 0.90).
- Guard 12.0 in plan / 12.6 off the band; funnel 2.1, plungers 2.0, below the opening 82.6 of 84; tip-over 29.8° at worst (left) on the feet vs DEFAULT 30 (the stance is held inside the 168 opening; alt wider feet need a wider funnel).
- Cameras: lenses 17.4 behind the L2 base plane at y ±75; rods block 0.4 % of each image; props, arms, motors and the L2 out of both views.
- Mass ~3.55 kg (E); 634 x 533 x 193 (rear prop tips to nose, feet to top); assembled box 690 x 597 x 245.
- **System line** (`python3 nox/assembly.py nox/overseer/overseer.assembly.json --check`, `runs/overseer_assembly/v1/check.txt`): cage first 9.5 mm, fit 0.0 mm³, balance 2.0 / 2.4 mm (pack in / out), probes +2.9 mm, access clear: PASS (`checks.txt` reads the same).
- Openings (DEFAULT): the L2 field; the pack swap path (it fills the 168 opening under the wheels, the cartridge walls take a side hit there; alt end bumpers need a wider funnel); the belly sensors (under the swap path, alt a removable guard bar).
- Hub joint loads (F, `hub_loads.txt`, `python3 nox/overseer/hub_loads.py runs/overseer_system/v2A`): chin 2069 N, front hub bottom 1419 N (15 N m), front hub top 1295 N, rear hub 603 N (17 N m), arm roots 759 N (109 N m), tower feet 278 N.
