What it is

  • Chamfered box frame round the camera, 0.5 clear at the sides, lips 6 above and below, all behind the lens. The board sits on 4 M2.5 heat-set bosses (28 × 28) with 1.0 TPU washers.
  • Lens hood: the frame runs on to x 153, 4.0 ahead of the lens. A flat hit lands on its front land and goes back through the cradle.
  • Faces are chevron trusses, the back is an X frame through the bosses, arms and knees are flanged webs in the cheek plane. Every visible face is planar and chamfered.
  • 4 octagon C-clips, bore 6.2, mouth 5.6, fingers 2.0, 9 long, mouths facing into the cage. Grip ribs hold the tube against rattle and slide. Two CA'd stop collars on rod 14-15 fix y.

Side section, to scale

Grid 10 mm. Cage rods projected onto the y = 18 plane. The hood and lips stay outside the camera's vertical field; the horizontal field is wider and clears the cheeks by more.

A is the simplest that meets every gate: one print, no hardware, push it on after the cage is built. Its fuse is soft. It lets go at about the force it took to push on (about 94 N straight, E), so an off-centre bump on the hood pops it. B keeps the camera on through frontal hits but adds 4 prints, 8 pieces of hardware and about 6 mm of lug height. Spark picks; B is drawn only on this page.

Open

  • Fit the clip on a coupon first: snap 0.20 per side and grip ribs 0.05 are estimates. Print one clip pair on a 6/4 tube offcut before the full mount.
  • Camera lens and board back are not measured (Q3). The field check takes the lens as a disk of r 6; it still clears by 30°.
  • For the cage: the frame model v2 put the bottom clips on 3.0 rods 14-16 / 15-16 (its v11 clip points) and weighed the 6/4 tubes at 3.0 g/m. With the real points and tube mass the lowest mode is 109.5 Hz, a cage pitch mode.
