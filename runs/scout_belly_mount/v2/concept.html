Scout v3: the FC on top, belly mount v2

Spark, 27 Oct: "the fc can live anywhere lets just do on top". The FC sits on the plate top at x 0, on the Bee35's own AIO holes, on 4 bonded silicone columns (lateral 111 Hz, E). The belly mount carries nothing of the FC now. It is a U-frame, 25.5 g (E), bolted through the Bee35's 2 side holes, so it needs no new plate hole. One requirement on another part: the tail block's foot tip is trimmed from x −14 to −20.

Attachments and wiring

  • FC: 4 silicone columns, M3 studs through the AIO holes into nuts epoxied under the plate; PCB clamped on the column tops by 4 M3 screws from above.
  • Belly mount: 2 M3 from the plate top through the side holes (3.9, ±31) into inserts; the receiver's 4 M3 up into inserts, unchanged.
  • Battery leads: straight up from the XT60 through a notch in the plate's nose edge, x 44..edge, |y| ≤ 7.5.
  • Motor leads on the arm tops; bay leads along the fin; USB-C out the +y edge; flow lead up the front strut and through the notch.
