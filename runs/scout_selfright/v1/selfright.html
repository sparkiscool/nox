Can the props flip the Scout back over?

No, not with props that only spin one way. Their thrust always points out of the drone's top, and that never turns the top back up. The current cage also rests stably in 6 poses: upright, upside down, on either side and on two rear corners. So something else has to do the flip.

Ways out

WayHowCost
Turtle mode (reverse spin)The flight controller briefly spins 1 or 2 motors backwards to roll it over. This is the usual answer. The Scout's AM32 ESC can reverse in firmware; PX4 has no turtle mode, ArduPilot does.No added weight or parts. A firmware change: ArduPilot, or a PX4 add-on. CHECK the ESC firmware.
Flip armA small servo lever on the roof pushes it off its back, then off its side.~15 to 20 g, one servo channel, a printed lever.
Self-righting cageReshape the cage so the only stable pose is upright: a peak on the roof, and sides that lean outward at the top.The sides must reach about ±170 at the top (now ±120). That means a wider, heavier cage and no race-car shape.

A roof peak alone (a roll hoop) only stops it resting flat on its back: it then falls onto its side, which is stable.
