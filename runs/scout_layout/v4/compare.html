Scout v3 cage, v3 and v4 (layout change after the review)

The review (PR 94) failed v3 on the crash probes, the third time in a row, so the layout changed: Radxa up 8 mm, shield nodes at the nose and tail bottom and the belly sides, receiver bolt key lines kept clear, reach paths checked (swap, bolts, FC USB), carbon judged by the length of each straight rod (no webs, no short carbon). v4 starts from v3 and adds only what the cases and probes need. Probe rows left: the receiver, cartridge and sliders seen from below (latch receiver v2 / cartridge v6 skids, asked) and the flow board at -1.4 mm.
