Scout camera clips

The camera cradle now snaps onto the finished cage. Each arm ends in a PETG C-clip that you push over a 3 mm carbon rod. No rod has to be threaded through anything.

Build order

  1. Build and epoxy the whole cage.
  2. Fit the camera in the cradle (TPU washers under the board screws).
  3. Push the 4 clips onto their rods: top pair first, then the bottom pair.

Concept, estimates (E). The snap fingers and the mount's first mode are set when the piece is designed. The frame model checks the mode is at least 100 Hz.
