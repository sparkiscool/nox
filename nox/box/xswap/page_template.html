Flip swap station

The pincer rides one vertical rail. A single squeeze on the shell's two latch pads swings both hooks off their ledges and grips the pack. The rail pulls it out, the pincer flips it 180°, and it drops into a bay that is the same holder print mounted upside down. Guide poles and a funnel take up a misaligned rail, and nothing but the pincer can open the latch.
