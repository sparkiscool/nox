Pincer strength options

v4's pincer had one weak link: each finger's arm was a thin 8 × 4.4 mm bar sticking out 23 mm, and pulling the XT60 apart bent it to 26 MPa, under 2× PETG's strength. It also couldn't be assembled: the one-piece jaw sealed the racks and pinion inside. All four versions below fix both, then add strength in different ways. The latch, shell and holder stay v4's.

Changed in all four

  • The jaw is a base plus a screwed lid (4 × M3 into heat-set inserts), so the racks, pinion and fingers drop in from above.
  • Each finger's arm runs right under the lid with a 14 × 14 mm pad round the post. A pull on the post pushes the pad up into the lid, so the arm no longer bends as a 23 mm cantilever.
  • Arm 10 × 6 mm (was 8 × 4.4). The 2 mm flange and the Ø7 post stay: they sit between the shell bottom and the gate bar, with 0.5 mm to spare.
  • Still 44 mm wide (the battery is 47). Every version passes the same checks as v4: no clash over the stroke at ±0.5 mm pincer error, both bars still release, the shell is held when the fingers are shut and free when they are open.
