Nox Ground Unit

The box that catches a drone, swaps its battery and charges it. One design for the Scout v3 and the Overseer. Every number carries its tag (P measured, M mesh, C CAD, E estimate, F frame model); the sources are the part docs in nox/box/ and nox/dimensions.md. The slot at the top is reserved for the catch the design center is designing (concept F, push bars on a flat deck; nothing drawn yet). There is no funnel any more.
