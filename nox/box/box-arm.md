## Wheel coordinates (W)
Origin on the axle at the stub flange's mounting face (the 2040 stub post's front face, `nox/box/box-stand.md` v5e). **Y** along the axle (+ away from that face) = the cartridge's length (z), closed end toward −Y. **Z** radial, up at the drone position. **X** tangential at the top = the cartridge's slide axis (X = −x: a proper rotation, B+ / B− on the two long sides; a wheel turn never mirrors them). phi = the direction head A's bar points; head B's bar points at phi + 90. Each head has its own frame: bar along +Z, glide inner end at u = 0; s = Y − Y_C.

## Head belt clamp v2: loop and pin (17 Oct 15:40, `nox/box/belt_clamp_v2.py`)
Each head's open GT2 6 mm belt runs motor 20T → idler → back, both ends held on the head's inner run (A: pitch line X 27.65, Y 66..72; B: X 48.65 in B's frame, Y 56..62). **Method from proven designs, not invented here:** Vampire Bat carriage clips (belt through a thin slot, wrapped round a 4 mm tube, squeezed to itself), goBILDA / ServoCity GT2 3-1 clamp (belt meshed to itself, one pinch bolt), printables 617391 (fold-in-loop, teeth against teeth). v1 (printed grooves + plate) is retired.
- **How it holds:** each belt end is folded back on itself **teeth in**, so the two legs mesh tooth into tooth, and pushed up a narrow slot; an **M3 screw through the loop is the pin**. Pull on the run tries to slide the legs against each other: the meshed teeth stop it, the slot walls keep them meshed, the pin stops the loop pulling through. No printed teeth, no plate.
- **Per end (C):** slot 12 long (6 teeth) x 6.4 across the belt width, 0.8 lead-in at the mouth; the legs converge over 3.6 to the pin; pocket r 3.23 round the Ø3 pin (wrap 2.88 + 0.35); pin hole Ø3.3 through both walls across the belt width; side walls ≥ 2.4, end walls 2.0; a 4 wide sight window on one side of the pocket. **Slot width = the coupon's pick** (1.8 / 2.0 / 2.2; meshed pair 0.63 + 0.75 + 0.63 = 2.01, E).
- **In the head (drawn feature, head chats):** both ends back to back on the inner run, mouths outward, pins 8.5 apart: 2 x (12 + 3.6) + 8.5 = **39.7 ≤ the 40 clamp zone** (A u −18.6..21.4, B −15.6..24.4, unchanged, `CLAMP_DZ`, `CLAMP_DZ_B`). The loaded leg lies on the run's line (no kink at the mouth). Pin = M3 x 12 + nut, or a Ø3 dowel. The joint to the glide's side skin stays generated (B: only at u ≥ −4). **Drawn 17 Oct 19:10 in `nox/box/belt_clamp_v2_head.py` (`head_clamp(h, slot)`, same keys as v1): slot centre X = pitch line − PLD − back + meshed / 2 (A 27.77, B 48.77 in B's frame), body X ±5.63 round it, Y = belt ±0.2 + 2.0 walls (A 63.8..74.2, B 53.8..64.2), pins at u0 + 15.6 and + 24.1 (A −3.0 / 5.5, B 0.0 / 8.5), mouths at u0 and u0 + 39.7; the slot width is an exact cut at build time (`arm_cad.CLAMP_SLOT`, env `NOX_SLOT`, DEFAULT 2.0) and the generator keeps out of 2.2, so one layout serves any pick; head A's low −X corner is trimmed by the 80T flange + 1 keep-out at park (8.7 mm3, C), gap 1.0 to the flange.**
- **Assembly:** fold the end teeth in, legs meshed; push the fold up the slot from the mouth until the loop shows in the window; push the pin through the loop; pull the run to seat it; trim the tail ~5 past the mouth. Length steps: re-fold one tooth along (2 mm of belt, 1 mm at the idler).
- **Belts (C/E, idler pitch taken as the 20T's, CHECK):** loop A 398, loop B 410. Each end uses 2 x (12 + 3.6) + π x 2.19 = 38.1 inside the clamp + ~5 tail, and the mouths are 39.7 apart, so **cut A ~445, B ~457**; cut 5 long, trim the tails.

Target pluck on the free return run (motor to idler, the run the head is not on): **A 143 Hz (span 179), B 139 Hz (span 185)** with belt 7.6 g/m (E); f = sqrt(T / m) / (2 L). Weigh the 918 spare piece to replace 7.6 (P).
- **Proof test before the heads print:** coupon set `belt_clamp_coupon_set_v2` (Nox Print Files `belt_clamp_coupon_set_v2.stl.b64.txt`; 3 coupons, slot 1.8 / 2.0 / 2.2 marked by 1 / 2 / 3 notches on the tab end; 11.6 g M; prints flat, no supports, bridges only). Pass = < 1 mm creep, no crack. **The narrowest slot that seats by hand and passes goes into the heads.**
