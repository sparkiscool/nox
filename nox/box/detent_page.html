    What the motion changed

    The roller never touches the 60° flanks. Their tangent points from the seated roller sit at r 23.75 (C), above the rim edge at r 23.5 (C), so the roller rests on the two notch edges. It seats at r 25.45 instead of 25.5 and climbs over the edge more gently.
