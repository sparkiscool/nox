## Latch return (17 Oct, `s2_blade.py`, sizing `blade_sweep.py`)
- One blade per slider, both rooted at the lead end, cantilevered along z under the floor (y 0.25..12.95, x ±4.7..5.0 at the root). The tip edge bears on its own tab's groove bottom 4.0 in from the tab's lead-end face (z 39.6). Free length 30.5, root at z 71.
- Preload 0.5 at rest. k 0.651 N/mm → **0.33 N at rest, 2.93 N at full squeeze (4.0), strain 0.22 %** (C; E 205 GPa). Same strain as V6. Gripper finger spring 8 N (E) has 2.7x margin.
- Sweep: root z 60..71, tip landing 1..4, preload 0.2..1.5; limits rest ≥ 0.3 N (pen-spring band), full ≤ 4 N (half the finger), strain ≤ 0.35 %, groove ≤ 1.5 (wall to the grip pocket); objective min full force. Both blades from the lead end: the closed end leaves ~23 free (stiffer).
- Checks (C/M, 15 squeeze states, sides independent): blade vs own tab, other tab, frame, tray, other blade; sliders vs frame and each other: all 0 mm³. Tip touching its groove at rest and squeezed. Clamp clear in the slot.
- Assembly: block glued in first; drop each blade into its post slot from below, push it up to the slot roof, check the tip sits in its groove, epoxy the clamp (scuffed). Let it cure with the sliders at rest (blade preloaded), then cycle.

## How it centres (insert, head pushing up)
1. **Capture:** the cartridge top meets the receiver's four post feet and two pocket-block feet at y 2 (44 below seat). 30° faces turn up to ±3.3 of x error into lateral motion (push x 0.58 sideways, E).
2. **Locate in x:** the guide rails (x ±20.0) slide up the receiver faces (x ±20.3) for the full 44 of travel.
3. **Locate in z:** the end tabs catch the end faces in the last 10 (±3.3 capture, then 0.3 per side).
4. **Contacts:** the drone's sprung shoes ride the rail chamfer and the ramp onto the copper strips (0.2 proud of the rail), `v2/s2-contacts.md`.
5. **Latch:** at seat the head opens, the blades push the bolts into the pockets, and the preload wedges them home. Pockets are 14.6 wide (±0.8 in z) because the receiver does the locating; the pocket only holds.

## Problems
1. **Mass: cartridge ~24.3 g (M/C) vs the 21 g line** (cartridge + latch + preload strip + camera mount, `v2/README.md`). The steel is 2.16 of it (V6 made the same trade: no creep, unlimited cycles). A 1.6 plate under the rail band would save ~1 g. Your call.
2. **Flimsy as printed (Spark 17 Oct 10:45).** v8 was an open section with no twist or squeeze case. Run 9 adds both and the under-floor band: v11 twists 0.90° at 0.2 N·m (C). v8's own number is unknown (its STL is on the PC): print v11 and compare by hand; if still soft, v11b (+1.1 g) or a top tie across the pack (shared change).
3. **Capture ±3.3 is an estimate of what's needed.** Measure the real stack in the swap coupon: funnel seat repeatability, wheel index, gripper play.
4. **Lead-end plate prints as a bridge** over the lead room (horizontal at the top in the +z print). The end bands are drawn and bridge 34. Check the first print; fallback: print the end band with tree supports.
5. **Something has to give for the capture (16 Oct 19:15).** Capture moves the cartridge up to 3.3 sideways, but the head is rigid and the drone is seated in the funnel. Fix (DEFAULT, E): de-energise the axle motor for the insert stroke (z), and mount the gripper on the head through a ±1.5 TPU float (x). Check both on the swap coupon.
6. **Contact before latch.** The shoes reach the strips 3.8 before seat; a bolt enters its pocket only in the last 0.25. A pack stalled in between is live and not latched. Fix (DEFAULT): the box head always runs the full stroke and the seat is checked after; the geometric fix (a deeper pocket mouth so the bolts catch ~4 before seat) is a shared change, Spark's call.
7. **Blade tip edge on PETG.** A cut steel edge bears on the groove (2.9 N max over 12.7, E). Deburr square; check the groove for a dent after 50 cycles. Fallback: a 0.3 printed shoe glued on the tip.
8. **Ceiling skin 0.95 over the blade tops** (block v4, z local 10..14, x ±0..5.4) carries the pack's floor load there. Fine at 6 g (E); check the print for a sagged bridge.
9. Tray v10 (patch of v9) is superseded by v11 (run 9); `tray_v10_blades.py` stays for reference.
