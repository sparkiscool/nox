# Scout v3 bottom layout v1 (24 Oct 2026): receiver v4, cartridge v4, pogo pads, underside stack

Spark 23 Oct: XT60 back, the contact pads are retired; the pack stays under the drone on the bottom latch. Every swap now mates and unmates the pack's own XT60 on the install axis (`nox/dimensions.md` I "XT60 mate": insert 60 N, pull-out 50 N, E). This part draws, places and checks the whole Scout underside in one sweep. Script: `nox/scout/v3/scout_bottom.py` (sweep, parts, checks, FE, motion). Run: `runs/scout_bottom/v1/` (`result.json` holds every number below, `motion.html`). Numbers shared with the box: `nox/dimensions.md` E (cartridge and receiver interface), G Catch interface (6) (pogo pads and KW11 target), H (underside stack), J (latch v4). Tags: P measured, C CAD, E estimate, spec maker data; **DEFAULT** = chosen without Spark, alternative in `nox/log.md`.

## Frames (C)
- **Drone frame** d (= dimensions G seat frame): x nose, y left, z up, CG at x 0, plate underside z 18.5.
- **Cartridge frame** c: x_c across the pack **= −y** (a proper rotation; dimensions E wrote +y, a mirror that did not matter while everything was symmetric; the XT60 key and the side-specific features make it matter now, DEFAULT), y_c up toward the drone (0 = flat bottom, at drone z −44.5), z_c along the pack (0 = closed-end outer face, at drone x 37.5). d = (37.5 − z_c, −x_c, y_c − 44.5).
- **Box W** (dimensions I): X = −x_c = +y, Y = 175 − x (tab line at Y_C 175), Z = z + 144.5. The +y side of the drone faces the 45° (Overseer) bay, the −y side the 135° bay.

## Problems
1. **XT60 dims are spec + E** (XT60E1-M insert and rear body, the pack plug's length, Q19, D17). First fit: plug the pack into the cartridge, tighten the set screws, push the cartridge into the receiver by hand: the XT60 must click home with the cartridge top on the strips. If the plug stops short: lower `Y_XF_FACE` (the shelf) in the script.
2. **Drone side +34 g.** Drawn plates for stiffness (Spark's v2 verdict); a generated v5 receiver would save ~20 g (E).
3. **Radxa hole rings** (o 7.6 part-free on the underside) and the 90° USB-C plugs are E: check on the board before the print.
4. **The v3 cage** (item 2) must keep its feet, legs and skids out of the post's landing cones and out of the side windows' plates: no drone point lower than a pin tip within the 45° cone (dimensions G (6)).
5. **FC corner clearance 0.84** at the side bosses (C on a sharp-cornered 36 x 36 board).
6. **Bays (bay module worker):** the cartridge is 93 long now (lead end at W Y 230.5 at Y_C 175, + capture 3), with a 3.0 set-screw boss block at the lead end below y_c 36.25; the bay copies the interface table and keeps the end chamfer zone free.
