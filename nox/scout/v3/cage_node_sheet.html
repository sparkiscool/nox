Both carry wall 3.0 round every bore and a 3.0 floor behind it. The floor of the mass is about 1 g per socket end (102 ends); the layout's 39 g was an estimate before any socket was drawn.

Breaking order

  1. A rod buckles (elastic, it springs back): 25 of 55 do in the 35 g crash (frame model v3, F).
  2. A rod snaps or its epoxy bond lets go (pull-out about 600 N E at 10 deep): re-glue or replace the rod.
  3. The node: sized to hold every rod's push, pull and full bending moment (1590 N mm) at the crash allowable, so it does not go first.

The TPU caps and the ring nodes take the landing; the camera mount's clips stay the camera's own fuse.
